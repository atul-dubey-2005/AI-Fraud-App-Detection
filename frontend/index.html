How FraudShield Detects Danger

Our multi-layered AI engine looks beyond the surface to identify malicious patterns before you click download.

AI Sentiment Analysis

Scans thousands of user reviews instantly to detect hidden complaints about scams, hidden subscriptions, or battery drain.

Permission Overreach

Automatically flags applications that request suspicious access, like a simple calculator app asking for your camera or SMS contacts.

Fake Review Spikes

Identifies unnatural surges in 5-star reviews generated by bots, commonly seen in fake OTT streaming platforms or unauthorized game top-up apps.

Developer History

Cross-references the publisher's background against a database of known malicious actors and previously banned developer accounts.
